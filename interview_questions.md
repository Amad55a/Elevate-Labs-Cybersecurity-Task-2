# Task 2 – Phishing Analysis: Interview Questions and Answers

## 1. What is phishing?
Phishing is a social-engineering attack in which an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organization to trick people into sharing sensitive information, clicking malicious links, or opening harmful attachments.

## 2. How can you identify a phishing email?
Look for suspicious sender addresses, urgent or threatening language, unfamiliar links, unexpected attachments, generic greetings, and requests for passwords or payment.

## 3. What is email spoofing?
Email spoofing is when an attacker falsifies sender information so an email appears to come from a different person or organization.

## 4. Why are phishing emails dangerous?
They can cause stolen passwords, identity theft, financial loss, malware infections, and unauthorized access to systems.

## 5. How can you verify a sender's authenticity?
Check the full sender address and original email headers. Review SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results. Contact the organization using contact details from its official website.

## 6. What tools can analyze email headers?
Examples include:
- Google Admin Toolbox Messageheader: https://toolbox.googleapps.com/apps/messageheader/
- MXToolbox Email Header Analyzer: https://mxtoolbox.com/EmailHeaders.aspx

## 7. What should you do when you receive a suspected phishing email?
Do not click links, open attachments, or share sensitive information. Report the email to your IT or security team.

## 8. How do attackers use social engineering in phishing?
Attackers exploit emotions such as fear, urgency, curiosity, and trust to pressure people into unsafe actions.
